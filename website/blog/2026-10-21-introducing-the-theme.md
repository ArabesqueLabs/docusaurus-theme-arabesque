The Arabesque design system now has a Docusaurus theme. It gives documentation the same colours, type and components as the rest of the system, and as arabesquelabs.com.

{/* truncate */}

The theme sits on top of Docusaurus's classic theme. Your docs, blog, sidebars and plugins keep working as they are; only their appearance changes.

## What changes

- Colours come from the system's tokens, in light and dark.
- Documents are set in Jost and code in IBM Plex Mono, from font files that come with the theme.
- Admonitions become notices: an icon and a word, on the kind's wash, with a rule at the start edge.
- Corners are nearly square, separators are hairlines, and there are no shadows.

Read [getting started](/docs/getting-started) to add it to a site.
