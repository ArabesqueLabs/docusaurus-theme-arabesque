# Writing style

The theme sets how the docs look. This page covers how they should read, from the design system's content fundamentals.

## Voice

Calm, precise, plain. Say what things are or do. Quietly confident, never hyped.

- **Sentence case everywhere:** page titles, headings, sidebar labels, buttons, tabs. No all-caps labels.
- **"We" for the team, "you" for the reader.**
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks.**
- **No superlatives or buzzwords:** not "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "next-gen".
- **Never invent facts.** No made-up customers, metrics or testimonials, not even as placeholders.

## Buttons and links

Buttons name the action: "Get started", "Install the theme", "Delete project". Never "Submit", "OK" or "Learn more".

Link text says where it goes. Write "read the [configuration guide](./configuration.mdx)", not "click [here](./configuration.mdx)".

## Errors and warnings

Say what happened and how to fix it.

:::danger
Enter a full email address, like name@example.com.
:::

## Imagery

No stock photos and no AI-generated illustrations. The pattern, diagrams and real product screens are the imagery. Never use mosque, dome, arch, crescent or lantern motifs, Arabic calligraphy as decoration, or faux-Arabic lettering.

## Checklist

Before you publish a page:

1. The title and headings are in sentence case.
2. Each heading level follows the one above it; there's one H1.
3. Every image has alt text, or is marked decorative.
4. Status is never shown by colour alone.
5. Admonitions say what to do, not only what's wrong.
